Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Enquiry Data & Privacy

When you submit an enquiry on this website, we collect your name, mobile number, and optionally your preferred project, plot type (residential villa or commercial), budget, and preferred site visit date. We use this information solely to facilitate your consultation, organize complimentary AC cab site visits, and share sanctioned blueprints, cost sheets, and MahaRERA documentation. We may contact you by phone, SMS, or WhatsApp.

Your details are never sold, rented, or distributed to third parties. If at any time you wish to remove your contact details from our records, you may notify us via WhatsApp or phone call at +91 98339 87246.

RERA & Project Listings Disclaimer

AnjaniPutra Properties is a specialized real estate advisory and consultancy firm representing sanctioned plotted township developments in Panvel, Khalapur, and Khopoli. Project names, MSRDC approved layout plans, 7/12 land titles, and MahaRERA registration numbers are shared directly with interested buyers upon enquiry and during guided site visits.

Plot dimensions, master layouts, starting prices, and possession schedules are indicative and subject to change per developer revisions and government approvals. Buyers are encouraged to independently verify all title documents and MahaRERA registration filings prior to executing any booking or financial transaction.
