Residential & Commercial Plots in Panvel

Panvel is the dynamic gateway connecting Navi Mumbai, South Mumbai via Atal Setu (MTHL), and the Mumbai–Pune Expressway corridor. With the Navi Mumbai International Airport (NMIA) starting commercial operations, Panvel represents the fastest-appreciating plotted real estate hub in Maharashtra (Mumbai 3.0).

Key Investment Advantages in Panvel:

  • The Riyasat Sankalp: 37-Acre Royal Plotted Township offering residential plots from ₹55 L to ₹1.70 Cr.
  • Sankalp Commercial Plaza: High-street retail and commercial plots with direct avenue access from ₹1.00 Cr to ₹2.00 Cr.
  • Riyasat Bliss: 23.82-Acre nature sanctuary with an iconic 65ft Shiva statue and wellness amenities from ₹78 L to ₹2.00 Cr.
  • Airport Proximity: Just 10–15 minutes from the terminal gates of Navi Mumbai International Airport.
  • Atal Setu (MTHL): Reach South Mumbai in 25–30 minutes with seamless signal-free transit.
